# Adjust: help center launch

<div className="margin-bottom--md">
  <a className="button button--primary" href="https://help.adjust.com/">Visit the Adjust Help Center</a>
</div>

The Adjust Help Center was a complete effort to redesign, rewrite, and reorganise Adjust's 
user-facing documentation, moving it from a simple static site generator to Salesforce.

The Help Center was planned, written, and executed by me and my team lead. The project also 
established the style, grammar, and formatting standards that were adopted across teams.

The live Help Center has changed since I left Adjust in 2018. The screenshots below show it at launch.

## Restructuring

The first task was to restructure the information architecture of the site. The old docs homepage 
was little more than a randomly arranged collection of tiles leading to unwieldy articles, which 
intimidated and confused users.

We decided that the information architecture should mirror the user journey through Adjust: first 
setting up their account and integrating the Adjust SDK, then configuring their ad campaigns and 
viewing their performance statistics, before finally acting on that information.

![The old docs homepage](/img/adjust-help-center/old.png)

![The new Help Center landing page](/img/adjust-help-center/new.png)

When pages were not completely rewritten from the old docs site, they were often broken into 
separate pages for each aspect of the feature. Here, the lengthy Campaign Wizard page has been 
divided into an introductory page and three sub-pages for the Help Center.

![The old and new (inlay) pages for the Adjust Campaign Wizard feature](/img/adjust-help-center/campaign-wizard.png)

## New and rewritten pages

A wealth of new content had to be created for the Help Center. This made up the bulk of the work, 
as only the two of us were working on this project.

Pages from the old docs site that predated my employment at Adjust were completely rewritten. 
For example, compare the “Callbacks” page on the docs site against the callbacks section on 
the Help Center (left and right side of the screenshot below, respectively). The necessary content 
was created from scratch, replacing an 11-section single page with 7 individual pages, providing 
more in-depth, exhaustive and navigable content.

![The complete reorganisation and rewrite of the callbacks section of Adjust’s documentation](/img/adjust-help-center/callbacks.png)

Plenty of new content had to be created for aspects of the product that were not covered on the docs site.

The most glaring omission was the lack of documentation on how to use the Adjust Dashboard, so a whole new section 
had to be created for this (the “Measure performance” section in the first screenshot below), as well as for 
SDK integration (see the second screenshot below).

![The first page of the new “Measure performance” section of the Help Center, which guides users through the Adjust Dashboard](/img/adjust-help-center/statistics.png)
